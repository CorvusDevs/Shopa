Features

Everything you need to find and share deals

Swipe Discovery

A full-screen card stack for browsing finds. Swipe left to skip, right to like. Fast, fun, and addictive.

Smart Feed

Browse deals with For You, Trending, Following, Nearby, and Newest sort modes plus category filters.

Photo Editor

18 filters, brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, sharpness, vignette, crop, and more. Make every find look great.

Collections

Save finds to custom boards with icons, colors, and access controls. Share boards with friends or keep them private.

Social

Comments, star reviews, likes, shoutouts, digital gifts, direct messaging, @mentions, and rich link sharing.

Search

Find items, people, stores, and tags instantly. Store autocomplete powered by Apple Maps.

Gamification

Earn XP, level up through 10 ranks, unlock 50 badges, complete daily and seasonal challenges, and climb the leaderboards.

Coin Shop

Nickname colors, avatar frames, banners, emoji packs, XP boosts, streak savers, mystery boxes, and more.

Bounties

Post a "Find It For Me" request with a photo, budget, and preferred stores. Other shoppers earn rewards for finding it.

Streaks & Spin Wheel

Maintain daily posting streaks with savers and calendars. Spin the wheel daily for coins, XP boosts, and mystery rewards.

Widgets & Siri

Streak and Find of the Day widgets for your Home Screen. Siri Shortcuts for posting, discovering, and more.

Privacy & Safety

Trust scores, AI content filtering, verified finds, photo metadata stripping, and a full moderation system.

How it works

Start finding deals in seconds

1

Snap a photo

See a great deal or hidden gem? Take a photo, add the store and price, and share it with the community.

2

Discover nearby

Swipe through finds in a full-screen card stack or browse the feed sorted by Trending, Nearby, or For You.

3

Save & collect

Bookmark your favorites into custom boards. Follow stores, earn badges, level up, and build your reputation.

Why Shopa

Built for real shoppers

Real deals, real photos

No affiliate links, no sponsored posts. Every find is a real photo from a real person at a real store. Verified by the community.

Works offline

Your 50 most recent finds are cached for offline browsing. Posts and actions queue automatically and sync when you're back online.

Free to use

All core features are free forever. The optional Shopa Pro subscription unlocks premium photo filters, discounted boosts, and exclusive cosmetics.

Requires iOS 17.0 or later. Compatible with iPhone and iPad. Available in 18 languages.
