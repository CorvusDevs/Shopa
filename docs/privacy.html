Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

Overview

Shopa ("we", "our", "the app") is developed by CorvusDevs. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

When you create an account and use Shopa, we collect:

  • Account information: Email address, username, display name, and profile photo.
  • User-generated content: Photos, posts, comments, reviews, messages, and collections you create.
  • Usage data: Interactions such as likes, swipes, and badge progress to personalize your feed and track gamification.
  • Location (optional): If you grant permission, approximate location is used for the Nearby feed and store autocomplete. Location is never shared with other users unless you choose to include it in a post.
  • Device information: Device type and OS version for compatibility and crash reporting.

Photo Metadata

Shopa strips EXIF metadata (GPS coordinates, device information, timestamps) from all photos before uploading them to our servers. This protects your location privacy even if you include a photo taken with GPS enabled.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the app experience (feed personalization, recommendations, gamification).
  • To enable social features (messaging, following, gifting, comments).
  • To send notifications you have opted into (social alerts, streak reminders, challenges).
  • To moderate content and enforce community guidelines.

Data Storage

All data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage). Data is processed in accordance with Google Cloud's security and compliance standards.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. We do not display advertising. The only external service is Firebase, which processes data on our behalf.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings within the app. When you delete your account, all your personal data, posts, and associated content are permanently removed from our servers.

Children's Privacy

Shopa is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can manage most of this directly in the app's Settings. For any privacy-related requests, contact us at corvusdevs@outlook.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at corvusdevs@outlook.com.
